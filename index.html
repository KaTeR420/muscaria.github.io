Muscaria formed in a damp rehearsal room under a record shop, built around the idea that a song could behave like a mushroom — mostly hidden, patient underground, then breaking the surface all at once.

The band draws on folk fingerpicking, doom-adjacent low end, and analog synth textures that sit somewhere between a hymn and a warning. Three albums in, they still record live, in one room, mistakes included.

Based out of a coastal town with more rain than sun, which, they'll tell you, is exactly the point.
